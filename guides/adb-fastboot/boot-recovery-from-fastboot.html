Boot into Recovery from Fastboot Mode

Type
Quick Guide
Level
Beginner
Time
~1 minute
Applies to
All Android devices with unlocked bootloader
Last updated
YYYY-MM-DD

When flashing custom recoveries, unlocking bootloaders, or fixing soft-bricked devices, you often end up in Fastboot Mode. Normally you would use physical buttons to enter Recovery — but if your buttons are broken, Fastboot commands let you do it from the PC.

Reference

  1. 1. Method 1 — Standard boot command (most devices)

    1. Open PowerShell in your Platform Tools folder.
    2. Verify the phone is detected:
    fastboot devices
    1. Send the recovery boot command:
    fastboot reboot recovery
# or for some OEMs:
fastboot oem reboot-recovery

    The phone will boot into Recovery within seconds.

  2. 2. Method 2 — Temporary boot into a custom recovery file

    Newer devices (Android 10+, A/B slots) may not have a dedicated recovery partition. You can boot a TWRP .img temporarily without flashing it:

    1. Download your device's TWRP / custom recovery .img and place it on your desktop.
    2. Open PowerShell and cd to that folder.
    3. Boot the image temporarily:
    fastboot boot twrp.img

    The phone boots directly into that recovery from RAM — nothing is written to storage.

  3. 3. Troubleshooting

    ProblemFix
    fastboot reboot recovery reboots to systemHold Volume Up while the phone restarts — most OEMs require this hardware workaround.
    command ignored or unknown commandTry fastboot oem reboot-recovery instead (older or OEM-specific devices).
    fastboot boot gives an errorThe recovery image is not signed or is mismatched to your device model.
