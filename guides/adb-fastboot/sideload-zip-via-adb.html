How to Sideload Flashable Zip Files Using ADB

Type
Step-by-Step Guide
Level
Intermediate
Time
~10 minutes
Applies to
All Android devices with Stock or Custom Recovery
Last updated
YYYY-MM-DD

When your phone is stuck in a bootloop or you want to manually apply an official OTA, and the device has no SD card slot, ADB sideload is the rescue method. It sends .zip packages (OTA updates, custom ROMs, Magisk root) from your PC to the recovery in one command.

Reference

  1. 1. Prerequisites

    • ADB & Fastboot installed — see setup guide.
    • Correct USB drivers for recovery mode (MediaTek / Qualcomm / SPD).
    • Flashable file — the OTA or ROM .zip to flash.

  2. 2. Step 1 — Prepare the zip on PC

    • Move the downloaded zip (e.g. update_file_xyz.zip) into your platform-tools folder.
    • Rename it to something short like update.zip or rom.zip.

  3. 3. Step 2 — Boot into Recovery

    • Connect your phone to the PC.
    • Open PowerShell / CMD.
    • Boot into recovery:
    adb reboot recovery

    Or power off and hold Power + Volume Up to boot into Recovery manually.

  4. 4. Step 3 — Activate ADB sideload on the phone

    On Stock Recovery:

    • Use Volume keys to navigate to Apply update from ADB (or Apply update from sideload).
    • Press Power to select.

    On TWRP / OrangeFox:

    • Go to Advanced → ADB Sideload.
    • Swipe the slider to start.

    The phone should now display: "Sideload started… Entering ADB sideload mode".

  5. 5. Step 4 — Send the sideload command from PC

    adb devices
# Expected: xxxxxx    sideload

adb sideload update.zip

    You will see a progress percentage in the terminal as the file uploads.

  6. 6. Step 5 — Complete the installation

    • When it hits 100%, you'll see Total xfer: 1.00x.
    • The recovery automatically unpacks, verifies and writes the zip.
    • Once you see "Install from ADB complete" or "Script succeeded", reboot:
    adb reboot

  7. 7. Common sideload errors

    ErrorCause & fix
    Total xfer: 0.00x (stops immediately)Corrupt zip, wrong firmware, or mismatched model. Re-download and double-check the model code.
    cannot read fileCMD can't find the zip — drag and drop the file into the terminal window instead of typing the path.
    adb: device unauthorizedAccept the USB debugging prompt on the phone.
    error: closedRecovery doesn't support sideload — try TWRP instead of stock recovery.
